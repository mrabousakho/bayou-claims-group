# Outline — Attorney Partnership Structure (for legal review, not a finished agreement)

If you pursue the attorney-led model, the shape that tends to survive Texas fee-splitting rules
(Tex. Disciplinary Rules of Professional Conduct 5.04) looks like:

1. **You are not paid a percentage of client recoveries by the attorney.** That's the fee-split
   the rule blocks.
2. Workable structures instead:
   - You are an **employee or W-2/contractor of the law firm itself**, paid a salary or flat fee
     for research/ops work, not tied to any individual case's outcome.
   - You **co-own the entity with the attorney**, and the entity is structured so the attorney
     retains control over legal judgment and client relationships (many states restrict
     non-attorney ownership of law firms entirely — Texas is one of them, so this path likely
     collapses into the employment structure above).
   - You run a **separate data/software business** (Option 2) that sells research access to the
     law firm at a flat, outcome-independent price — clean separation, no fee-splitting question.
3. Whatever structure you land on, get it drafted and blessed by the attorney's own malpractice
   carrier and the State Bar's ethics guidance before operating — this is squarely their
   professional liability, not just yours.
